## 1. Phase 1: Permanent Academic Indexing (Day 1)
* **Objective:** Give the technical paper permanent academic standing with a Digital Object Identifier (DOI) before distributing it to institutional targets.
* **Execution:** Follow the 3-step instructions in [zenodo_doi_instructions.md](file:///d:/projects/martian-cylinder/outreach/zenodo_doi_instructions.md).
* **Measurable Metric:** Permanent DOI minted and indexed on DataCite/NASA ADS.

## 2. Phase 2: Direct Engagement with Key Public Voice (Day 2)
* **Target:** Prof. Avi Loeb (`aloeb@cfa.harvard.edu`), Frank B. Baird Jr. Professor of Science, Harvard University.
* **Objective:** Deliver a courteous, data-backed correction to the $20\text{ cm}$ estimate he cited in his March 2026 article, replacing it with the verified $35\text{ mm}$ AA-battery scale and the 80–200 year dust clock.
* **Asset:** [avi_loeb_technical_memo.md](file:///d:/projects/martian-cylinder/outreach/avi_loeb_technical_memo.md).
* **Measurable Metric:** A reply, public citation, or updated Medium/Galileo Project note acknowledging the $35\text{ mm}$ photogrammetric scale and PDS telemetry audit.

## 3. Phase 3: Engaging Malin Space Science Systems & MSL Team (Day 3)
