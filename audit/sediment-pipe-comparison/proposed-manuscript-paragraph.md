# Proposed manuscript paragraph

A separate sediment-pipe investigation reproduced the target benchmarks and retrieved original PDS images for the Gale crater examples discussed by Rubin et al. (2017). Three partial-rim views were measured, while a matched two-termination and longitudinal-boundary comparison remained unavailable. Camera-projected circular and tapered segment models were evaluated. Review against earlier M4 data revealed that initial dorsal residuals used an extended tangent line. Refitting with finite-generator distances gave full-fit/guarded RMS of 0.7717/1.9363 px for the cylinder and 0.3946/0.7176 px for the frustum. The frustum satisfies the surface-tangency condition that the earlier M4 construction violated. Its endpoint sensitivity and the unvalidated burial and photometric scene constrain interpretation. The original geometric measurements remain reproducible, with physical origin unresolved.
