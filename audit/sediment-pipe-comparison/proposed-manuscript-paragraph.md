# Proposed manuscript paragraph

A separate sediment-pipe investigation reproduced the target's reported benchmarks and retrieved original PDS imagery for the four Gale crater localities discussed by Rubin et al. (2017). Three partial-rim views were measured, but none of the screened exposures supplied both terminations and a longitudinal boundary of one object for a matched joint comparison. A finite 3D circular segment projected through the retained CAHVOR camera fitted the target's selected coordinates at 0.3782 px RMS and predicted guarded heldout coordinates at 0.7201 px. This demonstrates conditional compatibility with a regular cemented segment, while its formation, truncation and burial history remain unresolved. Explicit visibility checks rejected one simple burial-plane configuration. An exploratory foreground-bank scene introduces additional terrain assumptions. The completed tests therefore preserve the measured geometric result without assigning a geological or manufactured origin.
