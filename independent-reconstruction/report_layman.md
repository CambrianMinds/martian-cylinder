# What the current investigation establishes

A small feature in a Curiosity image has two visible curved boundaries and a straight upper boundary that can be registered within a constrained geometric model. The study measures how closely those curves agree, then withholds selected points to test predictions beyond the points used for registration.

The results show manufacturing-like geometric regularity and support investigation of manufacture as a hypothesis. The visible arcs also permit other physical shapes, including a tapered segment. Complete shape, composition and origin remain unresolved.

Natural straight edges occur in the comparison images. The study has not measured how frequently nature produces the complete combination of boundaries observed here. The sediment-pipe examples inspected so far expose only parts of their geometry.
