# Current technical summary

The authoritative report is the [13 November 2026 technical manuscript](../papers/martian-cylinder-technical-paper.pdf), with [LaTeX source](../papers/martian-cylinder-technical-paper.tex). The prior long-form report is preserved in [the superseded-document archive](../archive/superseded-docs-2026-11-13/independent-reconstruction/report.md).

Thirty-five near-rim, 24 exposed rear-arc and 26 dorsal stations constrain a common-section, common-axis model with exact upper-line tangency. The strict image-plane M1 fit has 0.3788 px RMS. Frozen-shape rear registration predicts 18 withheld stations at 0.2084 px after six anchors determine translation. Guarded rear and three-family M1 predictions yield 0.2429 and 0.7233 px. Withholding the entire dorsal family yields 1.9092 px.

These measurements support manufacturing-like geometric regularity. The original dorsal objective uses an extended line. Finite-generator sensitivities and a physically tangent frustum leave the full 3D interpretation unresolved. The original M4 construction remains rejected for its tangency defect. The frozen-front rear-scale scan and jointly refitted frustum test different constraints.

The 28-image geological comparison measures straightness, with 835 qualifying windows among 226,426 and qualifying segments in 17 of 18 frames. It does not estimate the frequency of the complete three-family geometry. Partial pipe exposures remain incomplete comparisons. Composition, provenance and origin remain open.
