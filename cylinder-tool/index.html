How to Use the Martian Cylinder Tool

Direct On-Screen Gestures:

  • Toggle Handles (H): Press H or click the Handles button on the bottom bar to toggle all transform handles and bounding box on and off for a clean, unobstructed view.
  • Resize (Maintains Aspect Ratio): Click and drag any of the 4 corner box handles. Scales smoothly to any size while locking the aspect ratio.
  • Change Length: Drag the amber pill handle at the rear base (or use the Cylinder Length slider in the sidebar) to lengthen or shorten the cylinder independently.
  • 360° Angle Rotation: Click and drag the circular knob at the top of the cylinder stalk. Hold Shift to snap every 15°.
  • Move Position: Click anywhere on the cylinder body to drag it over any feature in your photo.
  • Perspective Tilt: Drag the diamond apex handle on the front face ellipse to match the 3D camera elevation angle.

Background Photo:

  • Click Add Photo or drag-and-drop any image file directly onto the window.
  • Press Ctrl+V to instantly paste any screenshot from your clipboard.
  • Use the Sobel Edge Filter switch to highlight rock and cylinder contour edges.

Shortcuts:

  • Space + Drag or Middle-Mouse: Pan viewport
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in / out
  • Arrow Keys: 1-pixel micro-nudge (Shift for 10px)
  • [ and ]: Nudge rotation by 1°
